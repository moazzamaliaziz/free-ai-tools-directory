## 🧭 How to choose the right AI tool

- **Start with your goal:** Decide whether you need help with writing, coding, research, design, video, or automation.
- **Compare options:** Review capabilities, limitations, supported platforms, and privacy policies.
- **Check pricing:** A product may be free, freemium, trial-based, or paid. Confirm the latest terms on its official website.
- **Verify before relying on a listing:** Tools, features, and availability can change.
